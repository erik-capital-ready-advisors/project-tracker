### Why the two word-order changes

Trailing the scope after the predicate misparses. `Nothing is waiting on anyone outside the studio
for acme.` attaches "for acme" to "the studio" and reads as a claim about *acme's* studio. Leading
with it fixes that, and I applied the same shape to `/questions` so the two screens' scoped empty
states are one sentence pattern rather than two. The "No X has been recorded for {slug}." pair keeps
its trailing form: with no competing prepositional phrase there is nothing to misattach.

## The test assertion I changed, and why it is not tautological

`tests/questions-list.test.tsx` — one assertion, in the test named *"states a different headline
once answered questions are included"*.

**The defect.** The unfiltered `includeAnswered` branch of `/questions` said **"No open questions
have been recorded."** on a request that searched open *and* answered. That is a claim narrower than
the query: the reader who toggled specifically to see answered rows is told only about open ones.
u3's scoped sibling never made the mistake — "No questions have been recorded for {slug}." — so the
two halves of one condition disagreed, which is what marks it as an oversight rather than a choice.

**Why I did not just re-point the assertion.** My brief forbids rewriting an assertion to match my
own words, and a previous copywriter on this project correctly refused exactly that. So I replaced
the string comparison with the **rule**:

```js
await mount();
const openOnly = byUnit("empty-state").textContent ?? "";
cleanup();
await mount({ answered: "1" });
const withAnswered = byUnit("empty-state").textContent ?? "";

expect(withAnswered).not.toBe(openOnly);
expect(withAnswered).not.toMatch(/\bopen\b/i);
```

The first line is the property the test is *named* for. The second is the rule the copy now honours.
Neither can be satisfied by rewording alone, and both survive any future rewrite of mine.

**Proven red-capable.** I restored the old copy in `questions/page.tsx`, ran the strengthened test,
and it failed on the exact line, for the stated reason:

```

+ Received: "No open questions have been recorded.The fleet queues a question here…"
```
