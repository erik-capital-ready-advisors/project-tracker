## Best-guess decisions

1. **`/stacks` is ledger-wide and does NOT honour `?engagement=`.** The FR-96 tripwire in
   `tests/engagement-filter.test.ts` fired on the new nav entry — it is designed to, and its own
   comment says "add a route to `nav.ts` and it fails until someone says which side of the line the
   new screen is on." **I said which side, in the test, rather than widening the expectation:** a
   named `DELIBERATELY_UNFILTERED` list now carries `/stacks` with a twelve-line reason, and a
   second new test asserts every entry in that list is a real route that really is unfiltered — so
   the exclusion cannot hide a typo or outlive its screen. The reason: `readStackRegister()` takes
   no argument by design, and FR-106 compares each row's **engagement count** against a threshold of
   two. Filtering to one engagement would leave the threshold in place while guaranteeing every
   stack shows at most one engagement — a filter that makes half a rule unable to fire, which is
   FR-96c's lie in a new shape. The screen states the picker's absence in words rather than leaving
   it to be read as a gap.

2. **FR-107's actionable treatment borrows `state-carried`, and this is flagged rather than
   hidden.** "Actionable stack" is not one of the five states spec 5a names. Amber keeps its hue and
   its valence (open, needs attention, not broken) and it is the **only** hue this screen spends —
   fuchsia stays reserved for `unparsed`, violet stays on interactive chrome, **no token was added
   and `state-badge.tsx` is unmodified**. This follows the precedent `@/components/answer-chips.tsx`
   set for severity, which is flagged as a borrow rather than a mapping for the same reason. **A
   decision Erik has not reviewed:** if he wants "actionable" to own its own ramp, that is two new
   tokens on a palette he already approved, and his call rather than a worktree's.

3. **`undetermined` and `settled` stay off the semantic scale entirely**, on the neutral ladder's
   `faint` (dashed) and `present` rungs. Dashed is what the scale itself gives `not-verified` and
   `unproven`, and an undetermined row is an absence of a finding rather than a finding. A scale
   that grows a colour per screen stops being a scale, and the first thing it stops being able to
   say is `unparsed`.

4. **No summary strip above the FR-108 panel.** `/runs` opens with one; here it would restate the
   blindness figures in a smaller font directly above them, which is the decoration FR-108 warns
   against. The `<Screen>` header still carries the title, the question and the requirement refs.

5. **The blindness grid does not render under `no-sessions`.** Nine zeros would restate an absence
   in the visual form of a measurement. The `EmptyState` copy carries the distinction in words
   instead, and a test asserts both the absence of the grid and the presence of the sentence.

6. **The agent-name input carries no `maxLength`.** The 96-character ceiling lives in
   `normaliseAgentCovering` and is returned as a sentence naming the actual length; a `maxLength`
   would silently truncate a paste and store a name short and wrong. It also keeps the module
   boundary at exactly the two imports the brief names — importing `AGENT_COVERING_MAX` would pull a
   `@/lib/server/*` module into a client component's graph. A test asserts the attribute is absent.

7. **The FR-106 rule is stated twice — once per screen, once per row — and both come from
   `register.thresholds`.** There is no literal `2` or `8` in any component. A test feeds the
   sentence `{ minEngagements: 5, minHours: 40 }` and asserts it changes, which is the control that
   proves the numbers are not hard-coded.

8. **Hours format as `7h 59m`, never as a rounded hour.** The whole subject of the screen is a
   threshold at eight; a near-miss reading as `8h` would put a stack over a line it is under. A
   measured `0` renders as a loud `0h`, and the "may understate" caveat is keyed on
   `sessionsWithoutDuration` rather than on the total being small.

## What was NOT done, deliberately

- **No engagement filter, no sort control, no pagination.** None is in CR-007 §3 and each would be a
  claim about ordering that `i1` deliberately refused to make.
- **No re-derivation of any figure.** `display.ts` formats; it does not sum, compare or count. The
  screen renders the order the read layer gave it and a test asserts the rendered order equals
  `register.stacks.map(s => s.name)` **and** that it is alphabetical — so a "helpful" re-sort would
  turn it red.
- **No second write path.** FR-109's is `i1`'s and it audits; this unit binds it and adds none.
- **No new §7a row, no new entity, no migration.**
- **`docs/user-guide.md` untouched**, per the brief. The route count moved 31 → 32, so
  `manual-gate.sh` will read 31/32 until `doc1` covers the screen. That is expected and budgeted.
- **Nothing is deployed.** This is code in a worktree on `agent-build/2026-08-24-9b85cd`. Not on
  `master`, not merged, not live.
